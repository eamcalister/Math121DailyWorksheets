    Handout 10.3 Friday Week 10 - Composition of Functions
    

    Objectives

    • Compose functions from simpler ones in practical and algebraic settings
      🔗
      🔗
    • Decompose complicated functions into a composition of simpler ones.
      🔗
      🔗
    🔗
    Many of the topics we have encountered in this class are a result of substituting functions into other functions. Indeed, graphical transformations of functions are the study of substituting linear functions into other functions (inside transformations) and substituting functions into linear functions (outside transformations). This process of plugging functions into one another is possibly the most important way of constructing new functions from known ones.
    🔗

    Definition 10.7. Composition of Functions.

    Given two functions \(f\) and \(g\text{,}\) the composition of \(f\) with \(g\) is ...
    🔗
    🔗

    Example 10.8.

    Let \(Y(q)\) be the corn yield, in bushels, when \(q\) liters of fertilizer is applied to a field. Let \(R(Y)\) be the revenue, in dollars, generated by a yield of \(Y\) bushels of corn.
    1. What does the composition \(R(Y(q))\) (also denoted by \((R\circ Y)(q)\)) represent? What are the units of the input and the output?
      🔗
      🔗
    2. Does the composition \(Y\circ R\) make any sense at all?
      🔗
      🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    Algebraic Composition.

    🔗
    Let \(f(x) = 3x^2\text{,}\) \(g(x) = 9x-2\text{,}\) and \(h(x) = \sin(x)\text{.}\) Find \(f(h(x))\text{,}\) \(h(g(x))\text{,}\) and \(\sqrt{g(f(x))}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗

    Decomposition of Functions.

    Often it is more important to decompose functions into simpler ones than build complicated ones from given simple functions. For each of the following functions, find simpler functions \(g\) and \(h\) so that \(f(x) = g(h(x))\text{.}\) Try to do each one in more than one way.
    🔗
    1. \(\displaystyle f(x) = (x+3)^3\)
      🔗
      🔗
    2. \(\displaystyle f(x) = \sqrt{1+\sqrt{x}}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    3. \(\displaystyle f(x) = e^{-x^2+1}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    4. \(\displaystyle f(x) = 9x^2 + 3x\)
      🔗
      🔗
    5. \(\displaystyle f(x) = \frac{1}{x^2 + 8x + 16}\)
